About the author

It's me, Zozor! I was born on November 23, 2005. My journey around the world has made me a citizen of the world and so it has become a habit for me to refer to myself as a citizen of the world because every place is home.

I'm a great traveller

It has been my life's mission to travel the world and see the different places around the earth. I can honestly say that I have done a good job at doing exactly that for the past 20 years of my life. A difficult by enjoyable journey it has been and for that reason I don't regret at all any encounters that I have been privileged to experience in these endeavours.
